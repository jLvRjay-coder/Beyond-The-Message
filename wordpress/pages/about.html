Our heart

Rooted in Scripture.
Made for everyday life.

Our mission is to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ and help people live out God’s Word. Through teaching, study resources, thoughtful conversations, and digital tools, we want to encourage faith that reaches beyond a Sunday message and into the way we live.

A message from the founder

When I first prayed about Beyond the Message, I knew it had to be more than another podcast or ministry project. I felt God calling me to build something that not only speaks truth but equips people to live it out — a place where faith and technology could meet to multiply the reach of the Gospel.

At its core, the vision is simple: one mission — to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Everything flows from that. We do this through discipleship teaching, leadership development, and creating spaces where God’s Word is lived, not just heard.

— Jamie Dennis
